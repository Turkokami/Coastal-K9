Virginia Opossum — Identification & Removal

The Virginia opossum is North America's only marsupial — the gray, cat-sized animal with a white face and bare tail. Opossums wander widely and den in whatever shelter is handy: under decks, sheds and porches, in crawlspaces and garages.

Quick answer: an opossum under the deck or in the crawlspace is usually a temporary visitor that becomes a resident if food and shelter stay available. Remove the attraction and close the den entrance.

How to identify opossums

FeatureWhat to look for
AnimalGray, coarse fur, white face, pointed snout, hairless ears, long bare tail
TracksHind foot with an opposable "thumb" pointing sideways
SignsRaided trash or pet food, gaps dug under decks and sheds, strong odor

Behavior and biology

Opossums are nocturnal, eat almost anything — insects, fruit, carrion, garbage, pet food — and don't hold a fixed territory. Females carry young in a pouch, then on their backs. "Playing possum" is an involuntary response to extreme fear.

Why opossums are a problem

They're generally harmless, but dens under homes bring odor, fleas and mess, and they get into garages and trash. Like any wild animal they shouldn't be handled.

Prevention you can do

  • Bring pet food in at night and secure trash.
  • Close gaps under decks, porches and sheds with buried hardware cloth.
  • Keep crawlspace doors and vents intact.
