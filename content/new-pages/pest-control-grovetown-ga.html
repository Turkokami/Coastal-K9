What drives pest problems in Grovetown?

Moves and turnover. Households arriving and leaving on PCS orders, plus rental turnover, move furniture and luggage — the way bed bugs travel. A K9 screen before you move in, or before you hand back keys, gives a clear answer room by room.

New construction on the edge of woods. Subdivisions backing onto pine woods see mice in garages, fire ants in new lawns, ticks along the tree line and occasional wildlife under decks and in crawlspaces.

Summer pressure. Stormwater ponds and shaded yards breed Asian tiger mosquitoes; seasonal treatment and dumping standing water weekly keep them down.

Who regulates pest control on the Georgia side?

Crossing the Savannah River changes the rulebook. In South Carolina, structural pest control is licensed through Clemson University's Department of Pesticide Regulation; in Georgia it runs through the Georgia Department of Agriculture's Structural Pest Control program and the Structural Pest Control Commission. Our Georgia work is done under Georgia credentials, and you can ask any company — us included — to show its Georgia company license before work starts.
