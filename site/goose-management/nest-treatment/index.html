Goose nest treatment

Geese return to where they hatched and raised young. Treating nests in spring stops new goslings, so your flock stops growing and the property becomes less attractive year after year.

Call or text 803-226-3155Email us

What nest treatment is

During nesting season we locate goose nests on your property and treat the eggs so they do not hatch. The adults keep sitting on the nest instead of laying again, which is why this works better than removing a nest.

Canada geese are federally protected. Nest and egg treatment must be done under the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service registration for resident Canada geese, and we help you complete it before any work begins.

Why it matters

  • Stops the flock from growing each year
  • Fewer goslings means fewer geese that learn your pond is home
  • Fewer aggressive nesting geese near walkways
  • Works hand in hand with K9 hazing

How the season runs

  1. Before nesting

    We complete the federal registration with you and map likely nest sites.

  2. Nest search

    We find nests early in the season.

  3. Treatment

    Eggs are treated humanely and each nest is logged.

  4. Follow-up

    We check back through the season and continue K9 hazing.
