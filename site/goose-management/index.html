Why geese stay

Resident Canada geese no longer migrate. A pond with short grass and clear sightlines is perfect for them, and they return to the same nesting spots year after year. Each goose leaves one to two pounds of droppings a day on walkways, docks and lawns, and nesting geese will charge people.

Decoys and noise makers stop working within days because geese learn they are harmless. A dog moving with purpose is a real predator to a goose, and they never get used to it.

How it works

  1. Assessment

    We map where geese land, feed and nest on your property.

  2. K9 hazing

    Trained dogs move the flock along humanely.

  3. Consistency

    Repeat visits break the habit so geese stop coming back.

  4. Maintenance

    An ongoing program keeps the property clear season after season.

Stop the flock from growing

Hazing moves geese along. In spring, nest treatment keeps new goslings from hatching on your property, so next year's flock is not bigger than this year's.
