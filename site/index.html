Why a dog?

A trained nose covers a room, a wall void or a warehouse aisle faster than any flashlight, and it does not depend on a rodent wandering into a trap or a bed bug showing itself. That means treatment goes exactly where the problem is, with less guesswork and fewer chemicals.
