Bed bugs · Treatment

Bed bug treatment that gets the eggs.

Killing adult bed bugs is the easy part. Treatment works when the eggs die too, so we match the method to the job and always confirm it worked with a follow-up inspection.

Treatment methods

Heat

Rooms are brought up to temperature and held there. Adults die quickly, but eggs need about 118°F held for well over an hour, so we monitor temperatures throughout.

Steam

High-temperature steam on seams, furniture joints, baseboards and cracks kills bugs and eggs on contact, without chemicals.

Conventional treatment

Targeted products applied where bugs hide, chosen with resistance in mind. Many bed bugs resist the sprays sold in stores.

Aprehend

A biological treatment that bed bugs pick up as they walk across it and carry back to where they hide.

How a treatment runs

  1. Confirm

    A K9 or technician inspection confirms bed bugs and shows exactly which rooms are affected.

  2. Prepare

    We tell you exactly what to do before treatment. For apartment residents who cannot do it themselves, we offer prep service.

  3. Treat

    Heat, steam, conventional, Aprehend or a combination, chosen for your space.

  4. Follow up

    A follow-up inspection is always included, about two weeks later, to make sure nothing hatched.

Questions

Why is a follow-up included?

A single missed egg can restart an infestation in about five weeks. Checking again is the only way to know a treatment worked.

Do bug bombs or store sprays work?

Usually not. Most bed bug populations carry resistance to common store products, and foggers do not reach where bed bugs hide.

Where do you treat?

Homes, apartments, hotels and businesses across South Carolina, including Columbia, Greenville and Charleston.
