Rodent control

Rodent control clears what is already inside while the building is being sealed. We place every trap where the dogs found activity, so control is fast, targeted and uses as little poison as possible.

Call or text 803-226-3155Email us

Targeted, not scattered

Scattering bait and hoping is how rodents end up dying inside walls. We set traps on the runways the dogs mapped, check them on a schedule and adjust as activity moves.

For mice we prefer unbaited snap traps set on runways. Rodenticide is used only where it is truly needed, which protects pets and wildlife from secondary poisoning.

Signs you need rodent control

  • Droppings along walls, in cabinets or pantries
  • Gnaw marks on wood, wiring or packaging
  • Scratching in walls or ceilings at night
  • Greasy rub marks along baseboards
  • Nests of shredded paper or insulation
  • A musky, ammonia-like smell

A control program
