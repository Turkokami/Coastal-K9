Rodent exclusion

A mouse fits through a gap about a quarter inch wide, and a rat through about a half inch. Exclusion closes those gaps for good, so the rodents you remove are not replaced next season.

Call or text 803-226-3155Email us

The step that makes it last

Trapping and baiting deal with the rodents already inside. Leave the way in open and you pay for the same problem every year. Our detection dogs show us where rodents travel, and our crew seals those points to a standard rodents cannot chew through.

Our exclusion crew includes metal work and concrete specialists, and our handlers have OSHA fall protection training for lift work on large buildings.

What we seal

  • Foundation gaps and cracks
  • Pipe, wire and utility penetrations
  • Rooflines, soffits and vents
  • Crawl space access and vent screens
  • Garage and dock door gaps
  • Dryer and exhaust vents

How exclusion works

  1. Find

    Dogs and technicians locate every active entry point.

  2. Seal

    Steel mesh, hardware cloth, sheet metal, concrete and durable sealants, chosen for each opening.

  3. Repair

    We fix damage rodents leave behind.

  4. Verify

    We check the seal holds and activity has stopped.
