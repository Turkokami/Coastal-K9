Rodent detection

Certified detection dogs find active harborage, nesting, runways and entry points inside walls, under slabs, behind product and along the property line, where traps and flashlights cannot reach.

Why detection comes first

Traps and bait stations only record a rodent after it is caught. A detection dog shows where rodents are living and how they are getting in, so every step after that is aimed at the cause.

Our detection dogs alert on all activity, old and new, which gives us the full history of a site. That is a big part of why our work in restaurants, casinos and food plants holds: the dogs find the way in.

What you receive

  • A map of confirmed activity and likely entry points
  • Photos of what was found at each alert
  • Findings set against your existing device map
  • Clear recommendations for exclusion and control

On site

  1. Pre-visit review

    We study your device map, recent trend data and any open audit items.

  2. Canine sweep

    Interior, exterior, production and non-production areas, voids, utility penetrations and the property line.

  3. Handler confirmation

    Each alert is confirmed and documented. Anything unconfirmed is logged as unconfirmed, not counted.

  4. Report

    A written report you can hand to an auditor.
